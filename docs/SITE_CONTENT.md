## Products ("Innovative Products")
### ViscoPhi™ (orthopaedics) – "Intra-articular viscoelastic implant"
* Intra-articular viscoelastic implant gel for joint disorders such as osteoarthritis.
* Prefilled, ready-to-use syringe.
* Features: Class III medical device · linear, crosslinked and combined hyaluronic acid forms · non-animal source · high viscosity and high molecular weight · MDR-approved CE.
### VisOcu™ (ophthalmology) – "Ocular Solutions" / ophthalmic viscoelastic device (OVD)
* Sterile, cohesive, isotonic, pyrogen-free viscoelastic preparation of highly purified hyaluronic acid (HA) and HPMC.
* Class IIb medical device · available in eight dosages · for lubricating, supporting and protecting ocular tissues during ophthalmic surgery (e.g. cataract surgery).
### PhilExir™ (dermatology) – "Dermal Fillers"
* Hyaluronic acid dermal filler range for aesthetic dermatology (facial volume, lines and wrinkles).
